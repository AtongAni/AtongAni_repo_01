# Rider Quick Start Guide

## Getting Started as a Rider

### Step 1: Register Your Account

1. Go to `/login`
2. Click "Register as rider"
3. Fill in your information:
   - **Username**: Your unique username
   - **Email**: Your email address
   - **First & Last Name**: Your full name
   - **Phone**: Your contact number (required)
   - **Vehicle Type**: Optional (e.g., motorcycle, car, bicycle)
   - **Vehicle Plate**: Optional (e.g., ABC-1234)
   - **Password**: Create a secure password

4. Click "Register as Rider"
5. You'll be automatically logged in to your dashboard

### Step 2: Accept Your First Delivery

1. On your dashboard, click the **"Deliveries"** tab
2. Click **"Available Deliveries"** sub-tab
3. Browse available orders
4. Click **"✓ Accept Delivery"** on any order
5. You'll see confirmation: "Delivery accepted successfully"

### Step 3: Track & Update Delivery Status

1. Go to **"Active Deliveries"** tab
2. Your accepted delivery will appear
3. Follow the delivery steps:
   - 🟡 **On the Way to Pickup** - Heading to pickup location
   - 🟢 **Picked Up** - Items picked up from sender
   - 🟡 **On the Way to Buyer** - Heading to buyer location
   - 🟢 **Arrived** - At buyer's location

4. Click **"📍 Update Delivery Status"** to progress through steps
5. Optionally add notes for each step

### Step 4: Complete the Delivery

1. Select **"Arrived at Buyer"** status
2. Click **"✓ Mark Delivery as Complete"**
3. Delivery is now complete!

## Dashboard Features

### 📍 Available Deliveries
- Shows all orders ready for pickup
- Each card displays:
  - Order number
  - Items and quantities
  - Customer info
  - Total amount to be delivered
- Quickly accept or reject deliveries

### 🚚 Active Deliveries
- Shows deliveries you're currently handling
- Visual progress tracker showing:
  - Completed steps ✓
  - Current step 🔵
  - Pending steps ⭘
- Update status with timestamps
- View delivery history

### 🔔 Notifications
- Get alerts when new deliveries are available
- See notification types:
  - 📦 **New Delivery** - Order ready for pickup
  - ⏰ **Delivery Expired** - A delivery was rejected (reopened)
  - 📍 **Status Update** - Your status was updated
- Mark notifications as read

## Tips for Success

✅ **Accept Deliveries Quickly**
- The faster you accept, the better customer experience
- More accepted deliveries = more earnings

✅ **Update Status Regularly**
- Keep status current for accurate tracking
- Add helpful notes (parking location, building access, etc.)

✅ **Communicate with Customers**
- Notes help customers know what to expect
- Clear communication reduces issues

✅ **Handle Items Carefully**
- Fresh produce requires gentle handling
- Keep items protected during transport
- Customers appreciate care taken with their orders

✅ **Be On Time**
- Manage your delivery routes efficiently
- Start pickups promptly
- Arrive at buyer location within reasonable time

## FAQ

**Q: How do I see if I've completed a delivery?**
A: Completed deliveries appear in your Active Deliveries list with a "Completed" status badge.

**Q: Can I reject a delivery after accepting it?**
A: Currently, you can only reject pending deliveries before accepting. Once accepted, you must complete it.

**Q: What if I can't find the customer's location?**
A: Note it in the status update. Add helpful details like nearby landmarks or building numbers.

**Q: How do I earn money?**
A: Each completed delivery earns you payment. Earnings will be calculat based on delivery distance/time (future feature).

**Q: Can I see my total earnings?**
A: Earnings dashboard coming soon! For now, track your completed deliveries.

**Q: What if there's an issue with the order?**
A: Note the issue in your status update and contact support through the app.

**Q: How do I cancel a delivery?**
A: Contact support immediately if you cannot complete a delivery.
